# Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics

Welcome to the comprehensive course book for **Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics**. This resource guides you through the exciting journey of building embodied intelligence—AI systems that interact with and understand the physical world.

---

## Why Physical AI Matters

The future of artificial intelligence extends far beyond screens and servers. **Physical AI** represents a paradigm shift where intelligent systems move from the digital realm into our physical world, understanding gravity, friction, momentum, and the complexities of real-world interaction.

**Humanoid robots** are uniquely positioned to excel in human-centered environments because they share our physical form. This biomimetic design allows them to:

* Navigate spaces designed for humans
* Use tools created for human hands
* Learn from vast corpus of human demonstration data
* Interact naturally in our world

This course prepares you to be at the forefront of this transformation—from AI models confined to digital spaces to **embodied intelligence** that operates in physical reality.

## Hardware Requirements

This course is technically demanding. It sits at the intersection of three heavy computational loads:

* **Physics Simulation** (Isaac Sim/Gazebo)
* **Visual Perception** (SLAM/Computer Vision)
* **Generative AI** (LLMs/VLA)

### Recommended Workstation

* **GPU**: NVIDIA RTX 4070 Ti (12GB VRAM) or higher
* **CPU**: Intel Core i7 (13th Gen+) or AMD Ryzen 9
* **RAM**: 64 GB DDR5 (32 GB minimum)
* **OS**: Ubuntu 22.04 LTS

### Edge AI Kit

* **Brain**: NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano (8GB) or Orin NX (16GB)
* **Eyes**: Intel RealSense D435i or D455
* **Voice**: USB Microphone/Speaker array (ReSpeaker)
